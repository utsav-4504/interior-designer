About

New name. Real experience.

MAN INTERIOR may be new, but interior design is not new to the person behind it.

The studio is founded on professional working experience in interior design — understanding client requirements, developing layouts, exploring concepts, working with materials, creating visualizations, refining details, solving practical problems, and navigating the many decisions that exist between an empty space and a finished interior.

Starting independently does not erase that experience. It simply gives it a new direction.

MAN INTERIOR was created to build a more personal, thoughtful, and focused design practice — one where every project receives genuine involvement and every decision has a reason.

We are at the beginning. And beginnings have one useful advantage: nothing is on autopilot yet.

What we believe

Good design should make sense.

Beautiful is good. Beautiful and functional is better. Beautiful, functional, comfortable, practical, and within a real-world budget? Now we’re interested.

We believe interior design should respond to the people using the space — not simply to trends, algorithms, or whatever shade of beige is currently having a moment.

  • Every layout should have a reason.
  • Every material should belong.
  • Every detail should contribute.
  • And every space should feel like it was designed for someone. Because it was.

Services

What we actually do.

Six directions, one approach: understand the space first, then make every later decision easier to defend.

01 · Residential

Residential interior design

Homes designed around real people, real routines, real storage needs, and the occasional chair that somehow becomes a permanent wardrobe. From individual rooms to complete residential concepts, we create spaces that balance comfort, functionality, and visual character.

02 · Commercial

Commercial interior design

Workplaces, retail spaces, studios, clinics, reception areas, and selected hospitality environments. Spaces designed to support business needs while creating a strong and considered experience. Because “commercial” does not have to mean grey carpet and one motivational quote.

03 · Planning

Space planning

Before choosing colours, finishes, or furniture, the space needs to work. We study movement, furniture placement, functional zones, proportions, circulation, and usability. Sometimes the most important design decision is moving something 300 mm. We take those 300 mm very seriously.

04 · Visualization

3D visualization

See the proposed space before implementation begins. Understand layouts, materials, colours, lighting, furniture, and overall atmosphere before making expensive decisions in the real world. Much cheaper than discovering later that your “warm neutral” is surprisingly orange.

05 · Renovation

Renovation design

For spaces that are not bad. Just… ready for a different chapter. We rethink layouts, materials, functionality, furniture, lighting, and visual direction to uncover new possibilities within existing spaces.

06 · Materials

Material & colour consultation

Materials are easy. Until you are looking at 84 laminate samples that all appear to be the same oak. We help bring clarity to colours, finishes, textures, flooring, wall treatments, fabrics, and material combinations.

Our process

Serious process. Minimal drama.

01 · Understand

We begin with your space, your needs, your lifestyle, your business, your preferences, your budget — and the Pinterest folder you have been quietly building for three years.

02 · Explore

We study possibilities through references, mood, materials, visual directions, and design ideas. Not every idea survives this stage. That is usually a good thing.

03 · Plan

Layouts come first. Movement, furniture, function, storage, proportion. Because a beautiful room you cannot comfortably walk through is technically an obstacle course.

04 · Visualize

The selected direction begins to take shape through design development and visualization. This is where the idea starts feeling real — and where everyone suddenly develops strong opinions about beige.

05 · Refine

We review materials, colours, details, finishes, practical requirements, and overall balance. Then refine again. Because “almost right” is still not right.

06 · Support

Depending on the agreed project scope, we provide design guidance and coordination support to help maintain the intended design vision during implementation. Because drawings are patient. Sites are not.
